Job Title: Senior Machine Learning Engineer
Department: AI & Platform Engineering
Location: Remote / Hybrid
Employment Type: Full-Time

About the Role:
We are looking for a Senior Machine Learning Engineer to architect, build, and deploy production ML systems and LLM applications. You will work on low-latency inference pipelines, automated model retraining, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) architectures serving millions of users.

Required Qualifications:
- 4+ years of production experience developing and deploying machine learning models.
- Expert proficiency in Python, PyTorch or TensorFlow, and NumPy/Pandas.
- Demonstrated experience deploying ML models to AWS (SageMaker, EKS, ECS, S3, CloudWatch).
- Strong command of SQL and relational databases (PostgreSQL/MySQL).
- Containerization and orchestration experience with Docker and Kubernetes.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, or related quantitative field.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with LLM orchestration (LangChain, LlamaIndex) and Vector Databases (Pinecone, Qdrant, Milvus).
- Production MLOps experience with MLflow, Kubeflow, or Weights & Biases.
- Experience with distributed model training and latency optimization (TensorRT, ONNX Runtime, Triton).

Responsibilities:
- Build, optimize, and maintain real-time and batch ML inference microservices.
- Design automated training, validation, and model drift detection workflows.
- Partner with product and data engineering teams to deploy performant AI features.
- Ensure 99.9% uptime, data privacy compliance, and low inference latency SLAs.
